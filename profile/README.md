## 🌍 What is Verana?

Verana is a **public, decentralized trust infrastructure** that enables ecosystems, organizations, and individuals to build verifiable services and networks they truly **own**.  

We solve three systemic problems of the internet:  

- **Trust Crisis** → no cryptographic identity built in  
- **Ownership Illusion** → platforms control your data and audience  
- **Discovery Monopoly** → visibility controlled by ads & algorithms  

With Verana, you get:  

- 🔐 **Cryptographic Trust** — every interaction is verifiable and authentic  
- 🗝 **True Ownership** — you control your services, data, and audience  
- 🧭 **Fair Discovery** — be found for what you prove, not what you pay
